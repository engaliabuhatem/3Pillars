## The distinction from HS Group

The current HS Group website presents HS primarily as an engineering consultancy with capabilities across the built environment, infrastructure, urban planning, and research and studies. It also lists feasibility studies, design services, construction supervision, and project management. 3 Pillars should retain the same sector relevance while leading with a more focused promise: project and programme management, business development, market intelligence, and strategic growth support.

## Recommended interpretation of the “three pillars”

The brief identifies two core specialisations: Project Management and Business Development. To give the name “3 Pillars” a meaningful and coherent platform, this blueprint recommends a third, supporting pillar: Strategic Advisory & Market Intelligence. This third pillar is a proposed positioning framework, not a confirmed statement of the company’s approved service portfolio. Management should validate it before publication.

| **PILLAR**                                    | **ROLE ON THE WEBSITE**    | **CORE VALUE**                                                                              |
|-----------------------------------------------|----------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 01\. Project & Programme Management           | Lead service line          | Plans, governs, monitors, and coordinates projects to support controlled delivery.          |
| 02\. Business Development & Market Expansion  | Lead service line          | Identifies opportunities, supports market entry, builds partnerships, and strengthens bids. |
| 03\. Strategic Advisory & Market Intelligence | Enabling pillar (proposed) | Turns market, sector, and project intelligence into better-informed decisions.              |

## Important content principles

- Do not present 3 Pillars as an engineering design company or a construction contractor unless that is part of its approved legal and operational scope.

- Do not copy HS Group claims, project numbers, office locations, client names, or track records as if they belong directly to 3 Pillars. Use group credentials only with clear attribution and formal approval.

- The service descriptions below are proposed website copy. Confirm delivery capability, contract scope, licences, geographic coverage, and the exact relationship with HS Group before publishing.

## Our Approach

We start by understanding the client’s objectives, operating context, constraints, and desired outcomes. We then define the right level of support, establish clear responsibilities and management processes, and maintain a practical focus on progress, risks, decisions, and results.

**3.** Coordinate action: align teams, information, decisions, and dependencies.

**4.** Monitor performance: track progress, cost, schedule, risks, and emerging opportunities.

**5.** Learn and improve: capture lessons, strengthen processes, and support the next stage.

# SEO Strategy & Keyword Framework

The SEO objective is to help 3 Pillars become discoverable for the services it genuinely provides and the sectors it genuinely serves. The content should prioritise high-intent service searches, sector-specific searches, and informative topics that demonstrate expertise.

| Brand                | 3 Pillars; 3 Pillars project management; 3 Pillars business development                           | Home; About Us; Contact                        |

## On-page SEO standards

- One clear H1 per page, reflecting the page’s main search intent.

- Use a unique SEO title and meta description for each indexable page.

- Use descriptive URLs with lowercase words and hyphens; avoid unnecessary parameters.

- Place the primary keyword naturally in the title, H1, opening paragraph, and selected subheadings where relevant.

- Use related terms naturally; do not repeat exact-match keywords unnaturally.

- Add contextual internal links between services, sectors, projects, and insights.

- Write unique image alt text that describes the actual image; do not fill alt attributes with keywords.

- Use descriptive calls to action that match the page and visitor intent.

- Ensure every page answers a distinct question and does not duplicate another page.

## Technical SEO checklist

- Confirm one preferred domain version (HTTPS and either www or non-www) with redirects.

- Generate and submit XML sitemap; maintain a correct robots.txt file.

- Use canonical tags for indexable pages and manage duplicate or filtered URLs.

- Implement mobile-responsive layouts and test Core Web Vitals and page speed.

- Compress images, use next-generation formats where supported, and lazy-load below-the-fold media.

- Add breadcrumbs on service, sector, project, and article pages.

- Implement appropriate Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, Article, and JobPosting structured data where eligible and accurate.

- Use hreflang only if additional language versions are launched and translated pages are properly paired.

- Set up Google Search Console and analytics with appropriate consent and privacy controls.

- Check 404s, redirects, indexation, broken links, and page titles before launch and periodically afterwards.

## SEO content quality

Do not publish thin sector pages that simply repeat the same paragraph with a different sector name. Each page should contain sector-specific challenges, relevant services, credible examples, useful FAQs, and links to related projects or insights. Prioritise useful information over word count.
